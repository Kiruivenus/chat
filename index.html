SAFETY & EXPECTATIONS

Before You Get Started

We strongly encourage all readers to follow these three basic guidelines when evaluating any online opportunity.

01

Research the Platform

Always check independent user reviews on platforms like Trustpilot, Reddit, and BBB. Search for recent experience reports to understand real payout timelines and policies.

02

Check Eligibility & Terms

Verify geographic restrictions, minimum age requirements, tax documentation rules, and supported payout options (PayPal, Wise, Bank Transfer) before spending time on applications.

03

Never Pay Upfront Fees

Legitimate employers and platforms never ask applicants to pay for jobs, starter kits, or guaranteed earnings. If someone demands payment or crypto deposits upfront, walk away immediately.
